## Project Context

WebHDP was developed as part of 3DBigDataSpace, officially described as
"3D Big Data for the Data Space for Cultural Heritage", funded by the European
Union under Grant Agreement No. 101173385.

WebHDP will continue to be developed as part of INFINITY, officially titled
"Multidimensional knowledge-based annotation for ethical context-aware heritage
data life cycles", funded by the European Commission under Grant Agreement
No. 101233051.
